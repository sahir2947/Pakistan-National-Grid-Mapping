# Sources of the places layer

The places layer tags every cell and sub-cell with its province, district,
tehsil and locality (see `pakgrid/places.py` and project.md §5.7). Places
version 2 is built from one packaged file, `places-v2.json.gz`, made by
`scripts/make_places_data.py` from two downloads:

| Level | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| Province, district, tehsil | UN OCHA Common Operational Dataset of administrative boundaries for Pakistan (HDX dataset `cod-ab-pak`, file `pak_admin_boundaries.geojson.zip`, layer `pak_admin3`), valid from 9 September 2022 | CC BY-IGO 3.0 |
| Locality | OpenStreetMap populated places of Pakistan, exported by the Humanitarian OpenStreetMap Team (HDX dataset `hotosm_pak_populated_places`, file `hotosm_pak_populated_places_osm_geojson.zip`), snapshot of 12 October 2026 | ODbL 1.0 |

| File (uncompressed) | SHA-256 |
|---|---|
| `pak_admin3.geojson` | `5535d348a720a9b0e5a0b79ee570408eff515419527d51cb5e16cabfd8699961` |
| `populated_places.geojson` | `1b770058066d0b7c71c41d17baae8ceb99d771fac08333636265c48f55eee5bb` |

`scripts/make_places_data.py` checks both checksums, so `places-v2.json.gz`
can be regenerated byte for byte. It keeps the tehsil outlines with their
names and P-codes (and their district's and province's), and the named
localities, with coordinates rounded to 10⁻⁷ degree, the grid's precision.

## What is in it

- **577 tehsils** in **160 districts** and **7 provinces and territories**
  (Punjab, Sindh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Balochistan, Gilgit-Baltistan, Azad
  Kashmir, Islamabad). Each tehsil names its district and province by
  P-code, so the hierarchy is the official one: there are no uncertain
  districts any more (places version 1 inferred them from coarse outlines,
  and got 79 doubtful, such as New Sukkur and Kot Addu).
- **32,512 named localities**: 29,137 mapped as points (23,766 villages,
  2,424 residential places, 2,040 hamlets, 727 towns, 155 cities, and a few
  neighbourhoods, suburbs and isolated dwellings) and 3,375 mapped as
  outlines (2,452 named residential areas, 370 villages, 227
  neighbourhoods, 152 hamlets, 97 towns, 37 suburbs, 17 cities and a few
  more). 13,418 have a name in Urdu or another local script as well.
  Unnamed places are left out.

## How they are used (places version 2)

- **Tehsils.** Every sub-cell that touches Pakistan is tagged with the
  tehsil containing its centroid. Along the edges the tehsil outlines stop
  slightly short of pakgrid's border (a different source), so 52,017
  sub-cells there take the tag of their neighbours. In a complete build
  every sub-cell touching Pakistan has a tehsil.
- **Districts and provinces** are the ones each tehsil names.
- **Localities.** A sub-cell takes the smallest named outline containing its
  centroid (a neighbourhood, residential area or village outline). Otherwise
  it takes the locality point with the smallest distance relative to its
  reach (10 km for a city, 4 km for a town, 2 km for a village, 1 km for a
  hamlet, neighbourhood or residential place, 500 m for an isolated
  dwelling). Otherwise it takes a town or city outline containing it, or no
  locality. 4,353,153 sub-cells (about a fifth of Pakistan, nearly all of
  the populated part) have a locality.
- A locality's tehsil is the one containing its point (for an outline, a
  point inside it), so its key reads like an address:
  `punjab/lahore/lahore-cantt/dha-phase-vi`.

## Known limitations

- **Coverage follows OpenStreetMap.** Villages are well mapped across
  Punjab, Sindh and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Neighbourhoods inside cities are
  mapped only in places, so much of a big city is simply tagged with the
  city (Lahore, Karachi, Faisalabad), and some neighbourhoods are points
  that the city's larger reach does not hide. Empty desert and mountains
  have no locality, which is correct.
- **Names are as mapped.** Spelling follows OpenStreetMap (the Latin-script
  name where one is given), for example `Lahore Cant`. Underscores and extra
  spaces are removed for display; the original is kept as `source_name`.
- **Tehsils are from 2022.** Tehsils and districts created since then are
  not in the data. Some tehsil names are spelt as in the source (`Liyari
  Town`).
- A newer download of either source makes a new places version. Grid IDs
  never change; apps keep their stored cell and sub-cell IDs and see the new
  names.

The locality tags in a grid database are derived from OpenStreetMap, so a
database that contains the places layer must carry this attribution, and a
modified version of it that is shared publicly must also be offered under
the ODbL. The `pakgrid` source code itself is not affected.
